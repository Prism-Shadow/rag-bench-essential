# Rubric: Harvey LAB Reps vs. Diligence Memo

Only the submitted `reps-vs-diligence-memo.docx` is scored. The memo is assessed
on three product qualities:

- coverage of the material discrepancy issues in the packet;
- recognizable binding to the relevant source files and SPA/schedule sections;
- practical severity, risk, and recommended-next-step treatment.

Official PASS requires all three qualities. The agent's research sequence,
reasoning trace, temporary notes, and helper scripts are not scored.
