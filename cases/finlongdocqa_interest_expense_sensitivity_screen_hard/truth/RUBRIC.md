# Rubric: FinLongDocQA Interest Expense Sensitivity Screen

Only the requested fields in `answers.json` are scored. They form three
independently checkable parts of the final screen:

- the included and excluded ticker universe;
- the complete ranking and its top, bottom, and spread;
- the reported shocks, standardized impacts, debt amounts, and ratios.

Official PASS requires all three parts. Source-reading order, citations, trace,
and intermediate notes are not scored.
