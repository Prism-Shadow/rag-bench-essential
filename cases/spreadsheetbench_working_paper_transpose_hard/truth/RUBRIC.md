# Rubric: SpreadsheetBench Working-Paper Transpose

Each of the three requested completed workbooks is scored independently. For a
variant to pass, `destination!A1:DQ8` must match its reference workbook and the
original source sheets must remain intact.

Each correct variant earns one third of the score. Official PASS requires all
three variants. The manipulation method, formulas versus values, trace, and
temporary scripts are not scored beyond their effect on the final workbooks.
