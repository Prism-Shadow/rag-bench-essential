# Rubric: Workspace-Bench Taobao Permissions

The three requested final artifacts are scored directly:

- `权限配置表.csv`: complete permission matrix and correct critical permissions.
- `权限校验规则.json`: parseable rules with `default_deny=true` and the required
  sensitive-operation role lists.
- `权限配置说明书.md`: substantive role and policy explanation, including the
  required price-visibility restriction for customer service and warehouse roles.

Each artifact is independently scorable for partial credit. Official PASS
requires all three. Trace behavior, source-opening order, and helper files are
not scored.
