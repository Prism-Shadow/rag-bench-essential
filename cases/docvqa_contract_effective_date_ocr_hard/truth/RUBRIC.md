# Artifact Rubric

Only `answers.json` is scored. `contract_effective_date` must unambiguously
represent July 1, 1999.

Accepted forms include `7-1-99`, `07/01/1999`, `July 1, 1999`, and
`1999-07-01`. OCR method, crop, bounding box, evidence, trace, and extra files
do not affect the score. A missing, malformed, wrong, or differently interpreted
date is FAIL; an ambiguous reversed numeric form is not guessed into the gold.
